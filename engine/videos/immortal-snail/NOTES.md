# NOTES: immortal-snail (daily run, 2026-11-17)

Shared code was not edited. For the owner and whoever maintains the manual:

1. **Topic.** The immortal snail is both a fresh row of today's trend file (a 53.9k-subscriber channel at 1.36M
   views/day) and an entry of the topic bank, so it was taken as a trend row backed by the bank. The trending videos
   are about the money; this film computes the chase.
2. **The one assumption everything rests on**: the snail keeps the pace of the fastest snail ever timed (Guinness:
   33 cm in 2 minutes) around the clock. That is generous to the snail (a garden snail does about 1 mm/s and rests
   most of the day), so "sixty-four years" is the least a flight across the Atlantic buys. The description says so.
3. **"One touch means game over"**: the thought experiment's usual wording is that the snail kills you; the film
   never says that. A red cross pops over the money sack instead.
4. **The snail is drawn cartoon-sized** (about 40 cm long) so that it reads on a phone; in the diagrams it is bigger
   still.
5. **Places.** "sky" is imported from plane-door-midflight (`places.ts`), with its airliner and iron weight
   (`props.ts`). "beach" is this video's own place (two planes and a foam line; the palm and the suitcase are cards).
6. **Sound.** There is no laugh in the vocabulary: the wife's laugh is a soft `crowd_cheer`. The beach uses
   `amb_ocean_night` (the calm-sea bed) by day.
7. **Sources.** Guinness, the Snail racing article, the USGS drip page and the CBP Houston release were opened;
   Murtagh et al. 2021, the APHIS article and the published JFK-LHR distances were seen as search results only
   (SCRIPT.md lists them). The film's distance is computed from the airports' coordinates.
