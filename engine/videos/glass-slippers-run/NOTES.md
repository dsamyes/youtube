# NOTES: glass-slippers-run (daily run, 2026-11-15)

Shared code was not edited. For whoever maintains the manual:

1. **Effect cards are drawn in the order they are pushed, not by depth.** A card pushed later covers an earlier one
   even when it is further from the lens (the elephants covered the slipper they stand in until the slipper was
   pushed last). The kit README does not say so.
2. **A hand lifted to the face is hidden behind the head**, so "she covers her eyes" cannot be staged; the wife
   screws her eyes shut and tilts her head away instead.
3. **The "sky" place is imported from plane-door-midflight** (`places.ts`), as are its `fxWeight` and `fxSweat`
   drawings. All diagrams of this film are flat cards in that sky.
4. **Frame checker.** Fast things that land on a word (the elephants, the weights, the ten-ton weight, the crumbling
   concrete, the bag tearing) are declared in `planned_pops`; everything else was eased until the proxy was clean
   (no pops, no near pops, no black or frozen frames).
5. **Sources.** The Saint-Gobain page returned HTTP 404 and the other documents were seen as search results only;
   SCRIPT.md lists them. The headline (1 cm cube, 10 tonnes) is one multiplication from the 1000 MPa that two glass
   makers publish.
6. Weak spots left: in shot 17 the first weights of the growing stack sit to the left of the bar until the row
   fills; in shot 18 Gus's hands are hidden behind the bag; the heelless slipper in the last shot is small.
